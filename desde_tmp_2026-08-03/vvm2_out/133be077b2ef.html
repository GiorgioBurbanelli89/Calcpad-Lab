Solution of triangle, defined by three sides

Lengths of sides:

a = 5  , b = 4  , c = 6 

Perimeter

P = a + b + c = 5 + 4 + 6 = 15

Half-perimeter - p = 0.5 · P = 0.5 · 15 = 7.5

Area (Heron′s formula):

A =    √ p ·  ( p − a )  ·  ( p − b )  ·  ( p − c )  =    √ 7.5 ·  ( 7.5 − 5 )  ·  ( 7.5 − 4 )  ·  ( 7.5 − 6 )  = 9.921567

Radius of inscribed circle:

r = Ap = 9.9215677.5 = 1.322876

Radius of escribed circle:

R = a · b · c4 · A = 5 · 4 · 64 · 9.921567 = 3.023716

Angles (Law of cosines):

α = acos(b2 + c2 − a22 · b · c) = acos(42 + 62 − 522 · 4 · 6) = 55.771134 °

β = acos(c2 + a2 − b22 · c · a) = acos(62 + 52 − 422 · 6 · 5) = 41.409622 °

γ = acos(a2 + b2 − c22 · a · b) = acos(52 + 42 − 622 · 5 · 4) = 82.819244 °

Type of triangle: acute, scalene.
